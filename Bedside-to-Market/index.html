Downstream
One fictional device, from a bedside observation to its first complaint. Six decisions, about fifteen minutes.

Every decision here is a bet placed with incomplete information, and the cost almost never lands on the screen where you made it. A choice made for legal reasons creates a testing obligation. A choice made for speed creates a use risk.

This case is invented
The company, device, competitor, clinicians and events are fictional, though modelled on how device development typically goes. Every number in the story — months, odds, complaint counts — is part of the fiction, not real-world data.
